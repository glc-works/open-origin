A family-run coffee estate in Pangalengan, in the Bandung highlands of West Java, at about 1,400 m. It has six main growing plots, a wet mill, a dry mill and a warehouse, and it also processes cherries bought from neighbouring farmers, sold as Frinsa Collective [1].
