Kartika 1 comes from a cross between Timor Hybrid 832/1 and Caturra. The material reached Indonesia in 1985 from the coffee rust research centre (CIFC) in Portugal. The Indonesian Coffee and Cocoa Research Institute (ICCRI) in Jember then selected it between 1987 and 1992 and released it for commercial planting in 1993. [1]

In global trials it combines good yields with strong resistance to leaf rust. It does best at high altitude, above about 1,300 m on Java, where its cup quality is good. [1]

**In Indonesia:** the rust resistance of Kartika 1 has weakened locally over the years, and the plant prefers high inputs. For both reasons it is now recommended mainly on Java and no longer for smallholders. It is one of the varieties often grouped under the everyday name *Catimor* or *Ateng*. [1]
