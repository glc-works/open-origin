---
title: Anaerobic fermentation
crop: coffee
type: process
summary: Cherries or beans are fermented in sealed tanks without oxygen, producing intense and unusual flavours.
---

In anaerobic processing, whole cherries or pulped beans are sealed in tanks, barrels or bags with the oxygen pushed out, often for 24–120 hours, before drying. Without oxygen, different microbes take over the fermentation and produce different acids and aromas.

The results can be striking: tropical fruit, cinnamon, liquor, yoghurt. Variations add yeast cultures, cherry juice, or temperature control. Done carelessly, it can mask the coffee's origin or taste overly fermented.

The method spread through competitions in the late 2010s and is now used by experimental producers everywhere, including in Gayo, West Java and Bali.
