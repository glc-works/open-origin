In the mid-twentieth century the United States Department of Agriculture collected coffee from Ethiopia and shared seed with research stations around the world. Some of this material reached Indonesia and is known locally simply as *USDA*, sometimes with a selection number such as USDA 762.

Because it comes from Ethiopia, the home of arabica, it brings genetic diversity that the Typica and Bourbon lineages lack. Farmers in East Java and Bali still grow it alongside S795.
