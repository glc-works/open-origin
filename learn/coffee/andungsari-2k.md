Andungsari 2K is a clone selected from a Catimor introduced from Brazil (genotype C-1662-10-3) and grown on the Kalisat/Jampit estates in East Java. The *K* stands for *klon* (clone): it is multiplied by grafting or cuttings rather than seed. [1]

The Ministry of Agriculture's variety guide gives an average of about 1.6 t of green coffee per hectare, up to 2.35 t, at 2,000 trees per hectare under single-stem pruning. Its cup quality is rated good, it resists leaf rust, and it is recommended above 1,000 m. [1]

It is susceptible to the root nematodes *Radopholus similis* and *Pratylenchus coffeae*, so in areas where they are common it should be grafted onto a nematode-resistant rootstock. [1]
