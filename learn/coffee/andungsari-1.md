Andungsari 1 was selected from a Catimor population that came from Colombia. Its exact history is unknown, but it probably descends from CIFC H-440, a cross of Caturra and the Timor Hybrid. The Indonesian Coffee and Cocoa Research Institute (ICCRI) selected it in East Java, and the Ministry of Agriculture released it as a commercial variety in 1999.

It is a dwarf plant, about 1.2 m tall when it starts fruiting at high altitude, so it can be planted densely. The Ministry's variety guide gives a potential of 3.5 t of market coffee per hectare at 3,300 trees per hectare, with an average of about 2.8 t. Cup quality is rated good, and the guide recommends it above 700 m. Its economic life is 10 to 15 years.

**Leaf rust:** sources disagree.

- **2018**, the Ministry of Agriculture's variety guide ([Randriani & Dani, *Pengenalan Varietas Unggul Kopi*, IAARD Press, 2018](https://jatim.brmp.pertanian.go.id/informasi-publik/download/varietas-unggul-baru/deskripsi-varietas-unggul-kopi)): resistant above 1,000 m, somewhat resistant below 900 m.
- **2025**, Royal Coffee's survey of Indonesian cultivars ([Evan Gilman, 2025](https://royalcoffee.com/indonesian-coffee-cultivars-and-varieties/)): susceptible.

Resistance in Catimor types often fades as the rust fungus adapts, so the difference may reflect the years between the two sources.
