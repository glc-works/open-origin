BP 358 was selected from the primary clone BP 25 on the Dampar estate in East Java. It is medium-sized, with a canopy about 2.4 m across, and yields about 0.8 to 1.7 t of market coffee per hectare at 1,600 trees per hectare. It suits land above 400 m better and does not tolerate drought. [1]

Like other robusta clones, it **must be planted together with other clones** for the cherries to set. The Ministry of Agriculture's variety guide describes its cup as typical robusta: neutral, without a sharp aroma. [1]
