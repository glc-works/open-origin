Libtukom is the liberica coffee of Tanjung Jabung Barat in Jambi, on Sumatra's east coast. On 6 December 2013 the Minister of Agriculture issued a decree recognising it as a variety under the name *Liberika Tungkal Komposit*. Locally it is often called *kopi excelsa*. [1]

It grows on the region's peatland, where few other coffees thrive. Mongabay reported about 2,710 ha planted across six subdistricts, producing more than 2,000 t of beans a year. [1]

**Cup:** the geographical indication certificate from 2015 records cupping by Indonesia's coffee research institute. A wet-hulled lot scored 82.75, with herbal and rubbery notes and high acidity. A honey-processed lot scored 83.5 and was described as sweet, heavy-bodied and very balanced. [1]
