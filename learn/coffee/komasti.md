The name Komasti is short for *Komposit Andungsari Tiga*. It is a composite variety: instead of one genetic line, it combines six genotypes, all bred by the Indonesian Coffee and Cocoa Research Institute (ICCRI). [1] It is one of the arabica varieties officially released by the Ministry of Agriculture. [2]

Growers value it because it fruits early, has large cherries, and resists leaf rust better than the original Andungsari. [1]
