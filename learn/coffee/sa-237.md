SA 237 was selected by ICCRI as tree number 18 in a robusta planting on the West Kendenglembu plantation in East Java. It was then tested in the Sumber Asin experimental garden, which gave it the SA in its name.

Like other robusta clones, SA 237 cannot pollinate itself: it **must be planted together with other clones** for the cherries to set. It starts bearing in the second year and suits agroforestry, with coffee grown under shade trees. It is highly resistant to leaf rust but susceptible to the root-lesion nematode *Pratylenchus coffeae*.

**Yield and fit:** yields vary widely, from about 800 to 2,100 kg of green coffee per hectare. It suits dry climates between 400 and 900 m.
