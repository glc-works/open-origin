BP 936 was bred by ICCRI by crossing SA 164-11 with BP 42 and selecting one seedling, labelled SA mb 54, which was released as BP 936. [1]

Like other robusta clones, BP 936 cannot pollinate itself: it **must be planted together with other clones** for the cherries to set. It starts bearing in the second year and suits agroforestry, with coffee grown under shade trees. It is highly resistant to leaf rust but susceptible to the root-lesion nematode *Pratylenchus coffeae*. [1]

**Yield and fit:** about 1,600 to 2,200 kg of green coffee per hectare (about 20% outturn). It adapts to many environments and does best in wet climates between 400 and 900 m. [1]
