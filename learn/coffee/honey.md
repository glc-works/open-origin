---
title: Honey
crop: coffee
type: process
summary: The skin is removed but some sticky fruit is left on the bean while it dries, a middle path between washed and natural.
---

In the honey process (also called pulped natural or semi-washed in some places), the skin is removed but the bean is dried with part or all of its mucilage still attached. The mucilage is sticky like honey, which gives the process its name, not any honey added.

Producers often name lots by how much mucilage they leave: white or yellow honey (less, faster drying), red or black honey (more, slower drying, more fruit). The result sits between washed and natural: sweeter and rounder than washed, cleaner than natural.

The process spread from Costa Rica and is now used by specialty producers worldwide, including in Indonesia.
