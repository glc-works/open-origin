Catimor is a cross between **Caturra**, a compact Bourbon mutation, and the **Timor Hybrid**. It was developed in Portugal in the 1950s and selected further in many countries. The aim was a short tree that can be planted densely, yields heavily and resists leaf rust.

Many Catimor lines exist, and quality varies widely between them. Grown high and processed well, it can make a very good cup; grown low, it can taste flat or bitter.

**In Indonesia:** the Catimor plants brought to Aceh and North Sumatra in the 1970s–80s became known as *Ateng*, with local selections such as Ateng Super and Ateng Jaluk. They are now among the most common trees in Gayo.
