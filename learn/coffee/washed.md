---
title: Washed
crop: coffee
type: process
summary: The fruit is removed and the beans are fermented and washed clean before drying, giving a clean, bright cup.
---

In the washed (or "fully washed") process, the skin and pulp are removed by a pulping machine soon after picking. The beans, still covered in sticky mucilage, are left to ferment in tanks or buckets for 12–72 hours until the mucilage loosens, then washed with clean water and dried to about 11–12% moisture.

Because the fruit is removed early, the cup shows the bean and the place more than the fruit: clear acidity, clean flavours, lighter body. Washed coffee needs water and careful timing, so it is usually done at a mill or a group processing unit.

**In Indonesia:** most coffee from Java, Bali (Kintamani), Flores (Bajawa) and Papua is washed. In Indonesian it is often called *olah basah* or *full wash*.
