BP 939 was bred by ICCRI by crossing BP 42 with SA 1366 and selecting one seedling, labelled SA mb 38, which was released as BP 939.

Like other robusta clones, BP 939 cannot pollinate itself: it **must be planted together with other clones** for the cherries to set. It starts bearing in the second year and suits agroforestry, with coffee grown under shade trees. It is highly resistant to leaf rust but susceptible to the root-lesion nematode *Pratylenchus coffeae*.

**Yield and fit:** about 1,400 to 1,900 kg of green coffee per hectare (about 21% outturn). It adapts widely but produces best in dry climates between 400 and 900 m.
