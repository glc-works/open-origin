BP 409 was selected from [BP 42](../bp-42/) on the Dampar estate in East Java. It is a fairly large tree, with a canopy about 2.5 m across, and yields about 1 to 2.3 t of market coffee per hectare at 1,600 trees per hectare, though its output is not stable from year to year. It is fairly tolerant of drought and somewhat resistant to the coffee berry borer and nematodes. [1]

Like other robusta clones, it **must be planted together with other clones** for the cherries to set. The Ministry of Agriculture's variety guide describes its cup as typical robusta: neutral, without a sharp aroma. [1]
