Gayo 1 and Gayo 2 are selections made from trees already growing well in the Gayo highlands. Instead of importing new material, researchers and farmers chose local plants with good yield, cup quality and adaptation to the area, and registered them as official varieties so that nurseries could supply them.

Local selections like these are one reason the same region can show such variety: each farm may grow a different mix of Tim Tim, Ateng, Gayo 1 and older trees.
