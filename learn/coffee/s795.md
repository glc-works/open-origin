S795 ("Selection 795") was bred at the Balehonnur coffee research station in India in the 1940s. It comes from crossing S288, a selection that carries some *Coffea liberica* genes, with the Kent's variety. It was one of the first arabicas to combine good cup quality with some tolerance of leaf rust.

**In Indonesia:** S795 arrived through the Indonesian Coffee and Cocoa Research Institute in Jember, East Java, and farmers often simply call it *Jember*. It is common in Java, Bali (Kintamani) and Sulawesi (Toraja), where it gives a sweet, balanced cup.

The name *Lini S* (S-lines) is used in Indonesia for this family of Indian selections.
