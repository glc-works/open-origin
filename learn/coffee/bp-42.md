BP 42 is a primary clone, selected from a mother tree in 1926. It is medium-sized, with a canopy about 2.2 m across, and yields about 0.8 to 1.2 t of market coffee per hectare at 1,600 trees per hectare. Its main value is as **the best pollinator** among the Indonesian clones, and as a parent of newer clones such as BP 409, [BP 936](../bp-936/) and [BP 939](../bp-939/). It does not tolerate drought and is susceptible to nematodes.

Like other robusta clones, it **must be planted together with other clones** for the cherries to set. The Ministry of Agriculture's variety guide describes its cup as typical robusta: neutral, without a sharp aroma.
