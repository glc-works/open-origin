# Content licence

All stories in `stories/` and photos in `photos/` are licensed under the
**Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence (CC BY 4.0)**,
unless a story says otherwise.

Some photos come from elsewhere, mostly Wikimedia Commons. They keep their
own licence (for example CC BY-SA 4.0), which the story records in
`photo_license` next to the author (`photo_credit`) and the original page
(`photo_source`). The site shows all three under the photo. When you reuse
such a photo, follow its own licence, not CC BY 4.0. Photos are resized for
the web and otherwise unchanged.

Full text: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode

When you reuse a story, credit the storyteller (`told_by` / `producer`),
the contributor, and Open Origin, and link to the original page.

The software in this repository (scripts, styles, workflows) is licensed
separately under the Apache License 2.0; see `LICENSE` and `NOTICE`.
