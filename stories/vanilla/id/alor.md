Vanilla (*Vanilla planifolia*) has been grown in the Alor Islands for almost fifty years, around Apui in South Alor. Alor is a dry island, and the local vanilla is recognised as a location-specific variety; a vine first flowers about 28 months after planting [2].

"Vanili Kepulauan Alor" was registered as a Geographical Indication in 2012 [3]. The protected product is cured vanilla: under the GI only dried Alor vanilla may be sold under the name. The growers' association, MPIG Kepulauan Alor, is chaired by Imanuel Langmau. In 2021 the regional office of the law ministry worked with the association to keep the GI from being revoked, because farmers had started selling green, uncured beans [1].

The story of Alor vanilla is still mostly untold. If you grow or cure vanilla on Alor, please tell it in your own words.
