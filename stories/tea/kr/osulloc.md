O'Sulloc began in 1979, when Suh Sung-whan, the founder of Amorepacific, started clearing waste land in the Dosun area, south-west of Hallasan on Jeju. He visited the site more than a hundred times and had rocks and scrub removed before planting. The result was a green tea field of about 3.3 million square metres, which became Korea's main green tea growing area as well as a place visitors come to see [1].

Today the tea comes from three organic gardens on Jeju: Seogwang, Dosun and Hannam. They hold organic certification from the USDA's National Organic Program and from the EU [1]. The name puts together the "Oh!" of admiration, the green of the tea fields and the snow on the Hallasan summit [1]. Visitors meet the brand at its Tea Museum and tea houses [1].

For forty years O'Sulloc was run as a division of Amorepacific. It became a separate company fully owned by Amorepacific Group, joining the group in September 2019 and trading on its own from 1 October 2019 [1].
