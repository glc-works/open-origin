Expocaccer, the Cooperativa dos Cafeicultores do Cerrado, is based in Patrocínio, the municipality with the most coffee in Brazil. It is the leading cooperative and warehouse approved to handle coffee under the Cerrado Mineiro seal [1].

Inside its warehouses, technology follows the coffee from the moment it arrives. Every lot that comes in for storage is cupped, classified and described in an online system: type of coffee, body, acidity, notes, harvest, and the exact plot of the farm it came from [1]. For a lot sold with the Denomination of Origin, a QR code shows the farm, the producers, the altitude and coordinates of the plot, the type of coffee, the harvest year and its sensory notes, and growers can see where their coffee went [1].

Under the region's rules, only coffee stored in approved places like this, from growers who belong to a cooperative or association linked to the regional federation, may carry the seal. [1].
