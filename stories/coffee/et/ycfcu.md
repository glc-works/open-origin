The Yirgacheffe Coffee Farmers Cooperative Union (YCFCU) was created in 2002. It brings together 28 primary cooperatives with 43 washing stations and more than 38,000 farmers, who support over 300,000 family members. All the cooperatives are in the Gedeo Zone of southern Ethiopia, and the farmers are Gedeo people [1].

In the union's own words, preserving the environment is part of their culture, and they combine traditional ways of farming with advice from agronomists [1]. The Gedeo Zone has about 62,000 hectares of coffee gardens and produces on average 15,500 tonnes of Yirgacheffe grade coffee a year [1].

The union works as the marketing body for its cooperatives. It arranges loans so they can pay for cherries at harvest, and it shares the profit back with its members. It has also built a school in Dila and bridges to reach isolated areas [1].
