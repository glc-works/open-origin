Frinsa Estate is a family-run farm in Pangalengan, in the volcanic Bandung highlands of West Java. Coffee was first planted there in 2011. Today the estate has six main growing plots at about 1,400 metres, and it runs its own wet mill, dry mill and warehouse [1].

The estate does not only process its own coffee. It buys cherries from a group of farmers around it, and once they have harvested, the estate processes their coffee too. Those lots are sold under the name Frinsa Collective [1].

For a washed lot, the coffee is dry-fermented, soaked, pre-dried on raised beds and finished on patios; depending on the weather, drying takes up to 20 days. The estate also experiments with lactic and anaerobic fermentation [1]. Its natural lots reach roasters abroad: Friedhats, for example, sold a natural from the estate as "Frinsa Manis #4", with notes of papaya, blackberry and orange [2].

This story was written from what the estate's buyers have published. If you work at Frinsa, you are welcome to tell it in your own words.
