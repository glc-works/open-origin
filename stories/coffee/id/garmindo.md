Gayo Arabika Mahkota Indonesia, known as GARMINDO, is a cooperative of 548 members in Bener Meriah Regency, Aceh. It used to be called ASKOGO [1].

Its members grow arabica on volcanic loam between 1,200 and 1,500 metres, with a mix of Bourbon, Caturra, Catimor, Lini, Tim Tim and Typica. There are two harvests a year, from March to July and from October to January [1].

The coffee is processed the classic Sumatran way: wet-hulled, which removes the parchment while the bean is still moist, and then dried in the sun. It is sold as Grade 1 and certified Fair Trade and organic [1].
