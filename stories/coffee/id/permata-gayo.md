Permata Gayo is a cooperative of more than 520 smallholder farmers in the Gayo Highlands of Aceh, growing arabica between 1,200 and 1,580 metres. It sells its coffee direct from the cooperative, without a separate exporter named in between [1].

Most Gayo coffee is wet-hulled, but the cooperative also makes a honey lot. The cherries are pulped and a measured amount of the sticky mucilage is left on the beans, which then ferment slowly as they dry in the sun. Trained cooperative members do the processing, with the cooperative checking quality. Buyers describe the cup as golden honey, soft florals and tropical fruit, with a silky texture [1].

The cooperative produces about 640 bags of this coffee a year on average [1].
