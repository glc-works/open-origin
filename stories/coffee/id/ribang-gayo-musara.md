Ribang Gayo Musara is a cooperative of smallholder farmers in the highlands of Aceh. Their coffee grows between about 1,300 and 1,650 metres, from a mix of Ateng, Bor Bor, Catimor and Timor [1].

Instead of the wet-hulling most Gayo coffee goes through, some of their lots are processed natural: the whole cherry dries around the bean. One of those lots, named after the farmer Asman Arianto, was roasted in New York by Coffee Bros., who describe it as cooked red grape, cacao and a syrupy sweetness [1].
